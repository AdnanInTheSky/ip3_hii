EVIDENCE-BASED METHODOLOGIES

Our Core MERLA Capabilities

Combining international gold standards with context-sensitive field execution across complex ecosystems.

Methodological Rigor

Globally Benchmarked Counterfactual Evaluations

  • Applying randomized control trials (RCTs), difference-in-differences, and propensity score matching to establish causal attribution.
  • Strict adherence to World Bank, OECD-DAC criteria, and FCDO evaluation principles ensuring international donor credibility.
  • Mixed-methods triangulation blending econometric analysis with participatory qualitative inquiry and political economy analyses.
Real-Time Telemetry

Continuous Monitoring & Results Measurement

  • Deployment of secure cloud telemetry platforms and geospatial dashboards tracking field delivery milestones in real-time.
  • Independent Verification Agency (IVA) services validating disbursement-linked indicators (DLIs) for sovereign loans.
  • Automated field survey quality audits with high-frequency check scripts detecting enumerator anomalies within hours.
Adaptive Delivery

Action Research, Pause & Reflect Learning Cycles

  • Institutionalizing structured 'Pause & Reflect' sessions that allow project directors to adapt strategy before midterm delays occur.
  • Predictive AI modeling anticipating project bottlenecks, procurement lag, and beneficiary drop-off rates.
  • Participatory community action research co-creating localized indicators with marginalized project stakeholders.
